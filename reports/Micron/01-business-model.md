# Micron business model, Duan view, 2026-10-19

Data cutoff is 2026-10-19. Source file `evidence/us-snapshot-MU.json`. Not investment advice.

Nasdaq labels the issuer `Micron Technology, Inc. Common Stock` on `NASDAQ-GS`, sector Technology, industry Semiconductors. Those strings are labels in the snapshot. They do not describe pricing power, customer concentration, or a contract.

Duan Yongping's test of a good business is whether the buyer can see a durable reason the customer pays. This snapshot does not contain that reason. The only operating trace that is safe to read is a set of scale-free margins from Nasdaq, one source, with no EDGAR tie-out.

- 8/31/2023 is -9.11197
- 8/31/2024 is 22.35275
- 8/28/2025 is 39.79079
- 9/3/2026 is 80.71598

Operating margin for the same period ends is -36.96911, then 5.19294, then 26.13837, then 74.5863.

A margin that moves from a large negative print to 80.71598 is what a commodity cycle looks like in a ratio. It is also what a changed product mix can look like. The snapshot cannot separate those stories. Judgment: the business quality is unavailable. The counterpoint is that the margin path is too wide to treat the latest print, 80.71598, as a normal year.
