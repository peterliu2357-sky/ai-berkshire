# Micron research note, 2026-10-20

Data cutoff is 2026-10-20. This is research, not investment advice.

Information grade is A. Micron has a long public filing record, and the trade press is crowded. That abundance is a bias toward the consensus, not a reason to treat the current margin as permanent. Investment certainty is a separate question and is low, because the ten-year earnings window still contains a loss year and the next contract-price print is disputed.

Evidence for GAAP, shares, closes, and the Treasury hurdle is `evidence/us-snapshot-MU.json`. Other figures are pasted from `evidence/source-ledger.json`. A bracket is the citation. The tier is inside the bracket.

Reproduce the issuer snapshot with the three commands in the domestic 10-K section of `skills/financial-data.md`. `snapshot` needs `SEC_USER_AGENT` in the environment. Do not commit that value.

What is not in the ledger: a three-scenario price, a ten-year IRR, a terminal-year profit, a NAND dollar price, an HBM dollar price, a currency label on the DRAM price table, a printable recent NVDA or AMD GAAP row, a TSMC GAAP row, and a Samsung local-share price.
