## Score

Judgment: the industry structure is three memory producers with heavy plants. The profit pool in the latest cited quarters is shared, which is what a cycle high looks like. The printable US peer rows are old and are not the current comparison. The moat that would protect a buyer is a price that survives a glut. The filing says a glut is what follows if HBM demand fades and wafers move back.

The counterpoint is the contract-price range that is still positive, and the long-term agreements mentioned on the Korean pages. Those are real constraints on the next quarter. They are not a ten-year price list.
